📊 AI Analyst Builder: Redefining How Teams Do Product Analysis
GenAI · Analytics Product Intelligence 18-Agent Pipeline Q2 2026

Problem Statement

Product teams spend weeks running analyses that should take days. A business question gets asked Monday. The analyst spends Tuesday-Wednesday exploring data, Thursday writing SQL and building charts, Friday documenting findings, and Monday morning delivering a presentation. By then, the business context has shifted, the question has evolved, and the insight arrives too late to act on.

Solution

AI Analyst v2 is an AI-powered research partner that handles the execution layer while you keep the judgment layer. You ask a business question. The system runs an 18-agent pipeline that frames your question into testable hypotheses, analyzes your data with automated exploration, segmentation, cohort analysis, and root-cause investigation, validates findings across four layers (structural, logical, business rules, paradox detection), tells the story with a narrative arc and Storytelling with Data charts, and builds the deck — a branded, presentation-ready slide deck with speaker notes and export-ready assets.

Business Value

⚡ Speed — Analyses that take weeks now take minutes. Insights arrive when they matter ✦ Productivity — One analyst becomes three. 80% less time on execution work ✦ Quality — Systematic, 4-layer validated methodology replaces intuition. Fewer errors reach stakeholders
